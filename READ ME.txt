-----------------------------------ABOUT---------------------------------------------------------------

Wibhage.lk is a online examination platform. Through this website,students can face to their examinations as usual before. 
A huge problem that we as students have faced with the onset of the covid 19 pandemic is the practical conduct of examinations. 
As a solution for this, students have the opportunity to conduct examinations successfully at home as before under the supervision 
of a teacher online as a live exam through wibhage.lk. Our platform consists of advanced features to achive this object.

WHAT'S SPECIAL?

*Create your own exam and let students to attend online.
*Attend to exam on a specific time remotely.
*Ability to complete exam within a given time range.
*Examine students in the exam with the ability to see and hear what they do.
*See what students do while the exam in their screens with "screen share" facility 
*Record the exam.
*Provide students to answer randomly chosen questions.
*Ability to see students' performace and ranks.
*Safety
*And more and more...

---------------------------Errors can be occured-----------------------------------------------------------

*Connection failed                   : Make sure database is correctly connected.In this case,mouse click won't work.If couldn't fix using any method, try modifying conn.php (PDO connection is must. mysqli connections won't work)
*fonts/css/links not working properly: Check your internet connection
*getUserMedia() error                : This may occur in exams with camera tracking.Give permissions for camera aand mic to solve this.
*Exam doesn't begin at the time I scheduled: This occurs if you are not in Sri Lanka or use vpn/tor browser.Time zone is hard coded as Sri Lanka because of localhost environment.If you are viewing site overseas,change your time zone in query/selExamAttemptExe.php line:10
*Can't scroll                        : This is not a bug,You may pointed mouse in another sub scrollable div. try scrolling on the right/left side.
*Sinhala characters not showing      :To avoid errors use English on localhost when creating accounts and creating exams.
*When I logged as user,cannot log into admin: This can be occured due to session cache.It's better to use seperate browsers to login as user /admin
